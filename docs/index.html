Method 1: Fastboot Flashing (PC)

Recommended for fresh installation from stock ROM or upgrading from LineageOS:

  1. Download and unzip VirgoX-Elite-GamingOS-v1.0-fogos-Android17-20260906.zip.
  2. Power off phone, hold Power + Volume Down to enter Bootloader mode.
  3. Connect phone to PC with high-speed USB cable.
  4. Run flash_all.bat (Windows) or ./flash_all.sh (Linux/Mac).
  5. Press Y to format userdata for a clean flash, and reboot!
